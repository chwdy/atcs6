## Logic Review Checklist

**How to use this checklist.** Each item below points to the single canonical home that defines the rule. Verify the implementation against that home directly; do not treat the wording of the checklist item as authoritative. If a question and its canonical home disagree, the canonical home wins and the checklist entry is the bug.

**Review checklist**

- **Controller reads dispatcher authority through the live projection.** Verify against the **received_path_plan schema** in [310-train-controller.md](310-train-controller.md) and the canonical projection rule in [200-path-dispatcher.md](200-path-dispatcher.md).
- **`Path.resource_sequence[*].path_tiles` carries directed entry/exit edges per the canonical schemas.** Verify against the **Path field schema**, **`resource_sequence` entry schema**, and **path_tile entry schema** in [200-path-dispatcher.md](200-path-dispatcher.md).
- **Lookahead is the single source of target speed and speed-limit reason.** Verify against the **canonical lookahead payload** in [310-train-controller.md](310-train-controller.md) and [400-data-flow-ui.md](400-data-flow-ui.md).
- **Manual-mode ATC enforces the live authority end as a hard stop.** Verify against `ATCComputer.calculate_authority_boundary_limit()` and the **Manual mode and movement-authority boundary** rules in [310-train-controller.md](310-train-controller.md).
- **Stop completion follows the Active-path completion procedure.** Verify the post-physics usable-authority stop check and the `PathDispatcher.try_complete_active_path(...)` requested-target check against the **Active-path completion procedure** in [030-core-loop.md](030-core-loop.md).
- **ATO handle calculation reads only the current tick's canonical lookahead.** Verify `ATOComputer.compute_handle(delta_seconds)` against the **ATO handle calculation** section in [310-train-controller.md](310-train-controller.md).
- **`RouteExecutor` reads live state through `TrainController`, not `Train`.** Verify against the **Route-executor state reads** section in [210-route-executor.md](210-route-executor.md).
- **`RouteExecutor._find_next_task_target(train_id)` resolves block-relative direction before calling dispatcher.** Verify against the **Planning Kernel** section in [210-route-executor.md](210-route-executor.md) and the dispatcher contract for `requested_destination_direction` in [200-path-dispatcher.md](200-path-dispatcher.md).
- **`RouteExecutor` and `PathDispatcher` share one current-span / candidate workflow.** Verify against `TrainController.get_current_span()` and the **`GraphWalkCandidate` payload** in [200-path-dispatcher.md](200-path-dispatcher.md).
- **`TrainPhysics` is the movement producer; `TrainController` writes the span; `RouteExecutor` and `PathDispatcher` read it through `TrainController.get_current_span()`.** Verify against [320-train-physics.md](320-train-physics.md) and [310-train-controller.md](310-train-controller.md).
- **Phase 1 covered-task index advance handles both `waypoint` coverage and same-block stopped-at-destination `stop` tasks.** Verify against kernel step `a5c6` in [210-route-executor.md](210-route-executor.md).
- **Explicit same-block / same-tile dispatcher movement follows the documented sub-cases.** Verify against `PathDispatcher.create_path(...)` step 9 in [200-path-dispatcher.md](200-path-dispatcher.md): multi-tile same-block accepts with or without a cab; same-tile requires a selected cab matching `requested_destination_direction`.
- **`create_path(...)` and `extend_path(...)` reject candidates with repeated resources.** Verify against the **`create_path()` flow** and **Path extension** rules in [200-path-dispatcher.md](200-path-dispatcher.md).
- **`best_effort` mode keeps one live `path_id` and extends `resource_sequence` in place; a truncated live authority is success, not failure.** Verify against the `best_effort` rules and the `result_kind` enum in [200-path-dispatcher.md](200-path-dispatcher.md).
- **`best_effort` fails (rather than storing live authority) when the head is on a switch with no forward block reachable.** Verify against the head-on-switch rule with `failure_kind = head_on_switch_no_block_authority` in [200-path-dispatcher.md](200-path-dispatcher.md).
- **Dispatcher callbacks identify active authority by `train_id`, not caller-supplied `path_id`; physical release after authority release resolves from the resource itself.** Verify against `PathDispatcher.on_controller_progress(report)` and the **Physical progress / deviation procedure** in [030-core-loop.md](030-core-loop.md).
- **Per-train runtime progress lives on `RouteRun` only.** Verify against the **RouteRun schema** in [210-route-executor.md](210-route-executor.md).
- **Operator retargeting / route swap is "abort + new assignment", never a `route_id` rewrite.** Verify against the **Route Edit And Reconciliation** rules in [210-route-executor.md](210-route-executor.md) and the procedure-ownership table row `d592` in [030-core-loop.md](030-core-loop.md).
- **Stop completion uses the post-physics usable-authority stop check; deviation cancellation flows as `path_cancellation` with `initiator = "deviation"` and a structured `deviation_kind`.** Verify against the **Active-path completion procedure** and the **Cancellation** subsection in [200-path-dispatcher.md](200-path-dispatcher.md).
- **`CAUTION -> FREE` reevaluation reads the canonical forward neighbour in `block.caution.exit_edge` direction.** Verify against the **`on_block_tail_vacated()` behaviour** section in [110-block.md](110-block.md). Completion-time `force` clear exception lives on `release_path_block_reservations(..., completed=True)` in the same file.
- **No completion or cancellation path unlocks a block or switch the train still physically occupies at full stop.** Verify against the terminal-teardown ordering in the **Active-path completion procedure** in [030-core-loop.md](030-core-loop.md) and the cancellation rules in [200-path-dispatcher.md](200-path-dispatcher.md).
- **After `TrainController.eb.release()`, the controller lands in `READY` (or stays `RUNNING_AT_STATION` for the dwell EB cycle) and restart goes through fresh path creation plus the canonical `TrainController.depart()` contract.** Verify against the **EB workflow state machine** in [410-end-to-end.md](410-end-to-end.md) and the **`TrainController.depart()` canonical contract** in [310-train-controller.md](310-train-controller.md).
- **Train-owned flank-protection claims carry `source_switch_id` so tail-vacate releases only the protection sourced from the vacated main switch.** Verify against the **Switch entity** flank-protection contract in [120-switch.md](120-switch.md).
